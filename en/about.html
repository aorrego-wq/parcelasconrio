How we work

Land with water, access and everything in order

Inmobiliaria El Roble selects land with water, access and natural surroundings in the Aysén, Los Ríos and Maule regions, and supports every purchase through to the deed.

  • Plots with their own property title, ready for the deed
  • Direct financing with the developer
  • Access by roads and rights-of-way
  • Site visits arranged with you
