El Fraile ski area: snow minutes from Coyhaique

Season, runs and how to reach Coyhaique's ski area, surrounded by lenga forest.

Updated: 15-11-20265 min read

In winter, Coyhaique slows down. The hills turn white, chimney smoke drifts upward and, less than an hour from town, a road through snow-laden lenga forest climbs to the slopes. The El Fraile ski area is a small mountain centre close to the city, with Patagonia as its backdrop.

This guide covers where it is, how to get there, its runs and lifts, when it opens and which details you should check before heading up, because several of them change from one season to the next.

Where El Fraile is and how to get there

El Fraile is 29 km from Coyhaique, up Cerro El Fraile on the road to Lake Pollux. About 12 km are paved and about 17 km are gravel or dirt, roughly 40 minutes by car. The ski area sits at around 1,599 m above sea level.

In midwinter the gravel section can be snow-covered. Go up in a suitable vehicle, drive carefully and check road conditions before you set off.

If you are coming from outside the region, the usual route is to fly into Balmaceda Airport and continue by road to Coyhaique. Our guide on how to get to Coyhaique explains the options.

Runs and lifts

El Fraile runs 2 surface lifts: a button lift and a T-bar. Do not expect chairlifts or large facilities; this is a ski area on a local scale.

  • Gradients: from 8° to 35°, so there is terrain for beginners as well as for those looking for something steeper.
  • Run length: from 600 to 3,200 m.
  • Number of runs: this varies by source. Chileanski mentions "5 runs" and Skiresort.info lists "5 km of slopes". Treat these as a guide.

Skiresort.info shows the area as "temporarily closed", without saying since when. Before planning your trip, confirm that El Fraile is operating that season.

Season and opening hours

The season generally runs from June to September, snow permitting. At a season launch, reported by Radio Revelación in an undated article, there was talk of extending it into early October. One package seller listed 5 July as the start of the 2026 season.

According to the same launch, the area opens Tuesday to Sunday: you drive up between 8:00 and 13:00 and come down between 14:00 and 20:00. Other sources give hours of 8:30 to 17:30. Since timings can change with snow and weather, check before you go.

Prices and management: check before you go

Caution is wise here, because public information is limited and not always current.

  • Reference prices: an undated listing shows a day ticket at CLP 27,000 for adults and CLP 22,000 for children. This is not a confirmed current price; use it only as a rough guide.
  • Management: the Radio Revelación article refers to the management of the El Fraile Mountain Centre under Ricardo Torres, and to the symbolic handover of the mountain refuge on loan to the Club de Montaña Campos de Hielo. On that occasion, governor Andrea Macías mentioned talks with the Ministry of Sport about a new management model. We could not confirm who runs the area today.
  • Contact: the phone number shown in listings is +56 67 221 3187, and the official website is reportedly skielfraile.com.

Before driving up, call or message to confirm whether the area is open, the day's hours, prices and whether equipment rental is available.

More snow: snowshoeing and winter in Coyhaique

Skiing is not the only way to enjoy the snow in Aysén. One operator offers snowshoe walks in 2026 at CLP 69,000 per person, with a minimum of 2 people. It is a good alternative if you do not ski or would like to walk through snowy forest at your own pace.

Winter in Coyhaique is cold, with frequent frost and snow. In August 2026 a cold snap and heavy snowfall led the municipality to set up an emergency shelter. If you travel in those months, pack thermal layers, check the forecast and bear in mind that rural roads can be difficult. Outside the snow season, the same landscape opens up to other plans, such as a Carretera Austral road trip or a visit to the Marble Caves.

Frequently asked questions

How far is El Fraile from Coyhaique?

29 km, about 40 minutes by car: 12 km of tarmac and 17 km of gravel on the road to Lake Pollux.

When does El Fraile open?

Generally from June to September, depending on snow, Tuesday to Sunday. Confirm each season's opening date before you travel.

How much is a lift ticket?

An undated listing gives CLP 27,000 for adults and CLP 22,000 for children. It is an unconfirmed reference price: check the current rate with the ski area.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Gradients range from 8° to 35°, so there are gentle sections as well as steeper ones. The lifts are surface tows, which is worth bearing in mind if it is your first time.
