Lake Elizalde: a visitor's guide

How to reach Lake Elizalde from Coyhaique, fishing, boating and things to do at one of the lakes closest to town.

Updated: 15-11-20265 min read

On a calm day, Lake Elizalde turns a deep blue, framed by green forest and slopes that drop almost straight into the water. A boat pulls away from a wooden pier and leaves a long wake across the lake. All of this lies about 33 km from Coyhaique, which makes Elizalde an ideal day trip from the regional capital.

This visitor's guide to Lake Elizalde covers what you need to know: where it is, how to get there from Coyhaique, what to do on the lake, what to see along the way and when to go.

Where is Lake Elizalde?

The lake lies in the municipality of Coyhaique, in Chile's Aysén Region. It is one of the three sectors of Cerro Castillo National Park, alongside Lago Paloma and Cerro Castillo. The park was created as a national reserve in 1970, became a national park in 2018 and, according to CONAF, protects 143,502 ha. In 2025 it joined the IUCN Green List.

One useful detail before you go: not all of the shoreline is park land. Chile's environmental regulator (SMA) reports private properties along the shore, including a subdivision that overlaps the park boundary. Respect fences and private access points, and ask before walking down to the water if you are unsure.

According to Interpatagonia, the lake is roughly 25 km long, with mostly steep shores and a few terraces that open up wide views.

How to get to Lake Elizalde from Coyhaique

The lake is about 33 km from Coyhaique, according to Lonely Planet and Interpatagonia. Interpatagonia describes the route like this:

  1. Leave town along Avenida Simpson towards the Simpson River and cross the suspension bridge.
  2. Head towards Teniente Vidal airfield. At the first junction, about 12 km from Coyhaique, turn left following the "CEA" sign.
  3. About 4 km later you reach the Seis Lagunas area. The road continues towards La Paloma, with a turn-off to Lake Elizalde.

On the way back you can loop through El Blanco and then join the paved road. Many guides also use the Valle Simpson turn-off on the Carretera Austral; we cover it in our Coyhaique to Cerro Castillo road trip guide.

Interpatagonia recommends carrying a good map, because some junctions are unsigned. Download an offline map before you leave Coyhaique.

Without a car, Lonely Planet says buses leave from the Coyhaique bus terminal. We could not find current timetables, so check at the terminal.

Things to do at Lake Elizalde

Fishing

Elizalde has a strong reputation among anglers. Interpatagonia calls it an "anglers' paradise" for its trout, and local operators highlight its fly fishing. On Aysén's lakes, Sernapesca allows anglers to keep up to 3 fish or 15 kg per person per day between 10 November 2026 and 1 April 2027; outside that window, catch and release is mandatory. You need a licence, available at pescarecreativa.sernapesca.cl. Read the details in our guide to fly fishing in Aysén.

Kayaking, boats and the beach

Lonely Planet recommends the lake for trout fishing, kayaking or a day on the beach. According to Interpatagonia, boats, bikes and horses can be rented in season at the campsite and the lodge, and there are guided trips on the lake.

Boca de León

Where the León River flows into the lake, a wide beach has formed, with meadows and native forest. This is where families spend the day, according to Interpatagonia.

Along the way: Seis Lagunas and a hydro plant

The drive is as rewarding as the destination. Tours of the Seis Lagunas (Six Lagoons) circuit stop at the El León waterfall, Laguna Verde, Laguna El Espejo and Laguna Burgos (according to a tour operator's catalogue). Interpatagonia suggests setting aside a full summer day for it.

A curious fact: the turbine house of the Lago Atravesado hydroelectric plant sits on the shore of Elizalde. The plant takes water from Lago Atravesado and pipes it down to Elizalde. According to Chile's Ministry of Energy (2023), it generates 40 to 42 GWh a year, around a third of Aysén's medium-sized power grid.

When to go and where to stay

The best time is summer. There is no official source with dates, but boat and horse rentals run "in season" and guides recommend the circuit in the warmer months. Outside summer, pack extra layers: the weather in Aysén changes fast.

Interpatagonia mentions cabins, campsites, a lodge and a fishing association in the area. We could not find current capacities or prices, so book ahead and confirm directly with each place. Because the lake is close to Coyhaique, you can also stay in town and go for the day.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Lake Elizalde from Coyhaique?

About 33 km, according to Lonely Planet and Interpatagonia. Part of the road is rural and some junctions are unsigned.

Is Lake Elizalde inside a national park?

The Lago Elizalde sector is part of Cerro Castillo National Park, but there is private property along the shore. Not all of it is park land.

Can you rent boats?

According to Interpatagonia, boats, bikes and horses can be rented in season at the campsite and the lodge. Confirm availability before you go.

Can you fish at Lake Elizalde?

Yes, with a Sernapesca recreational fishing licence and following the current season and bag limits for Aysén's lakes.
