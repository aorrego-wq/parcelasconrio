Aysén Region · Chilean Patagonia

A land to fall in love with every season

Glaciers, turquoise lakes, world-class fishing rivers, snow and endless forests. This is what awaits you in the Aysén Region.

Getting there

Coyhaique, closer than ever

Since 5 October 2026 Balmaceda Airport has operated a brand-new terminal: five times larger, with five jet bridges and capacity for over one million passengers a year. It is a direct flight of about 2 h 20 min from Santiago, then some 55 km by road to Coyhaique.

Must see

The Marble Caves

About 220 km south of Coyhaique, at Puerto Río Tranquilo, Lake General Carrera has carved caves into marble some 300 million years old. You explore them by boat or kayak, and they have been a protected Nature Sanctuary since 1994.

Scenic route

Carretera Austral

Route 7 links villages, glaciers, lakes and forests across Chilean Patagonia. In 2026 the Ministry of Public Works announced a plan of around CLP 800 billion to keep paving it through 2030.

Winter

Snow at El Fraile

Just 29 km from Coyhaique, the El Fraile ski area offers snow among lenga forests, usually from June to September. Ski or go snowshoeing without leaving the region.

Fishing

Fly fishing

Aysén’s 2026–2027 recreational fishing season runs from 12 October to 6 May. Rivers such as the Simpson, the Mañihuales and the Emperador Guillermo are famous for their rainbow and brown trout.

Hiking

Trekking and glaciers

Cerro Castillo National Park, south of Coyhaique, is home to the Las Horquetas trek: 53.5 km over 3 to 5 days. Further north, the Hanging Glacier of Queulat National Park drops towards Laguna Témpanos.

Living in Patagonia

Living in Coyhaique

Coyhaique has 57,823 residents (2024 Census), a regional hospital, universities and growing retail, surrounded by nature reserves. A town with services and the wild just minutes away.
