Before the plane touches down, the window is already full of Patagonia: snow-capped hills, rivers threading through forest, open country in every direction. If you are working out how to get to Coyhaique, the short answer is to fly to Balmaceda and finish the trip by road. What is new is where you land: since 5 October 2026, a brand-new passenger terminal has been operating at Balmaceda Airport, Coyhaique's commercial airport.

This guide covers what changed with the new terminal, which flights currently arrive from Santiago and Puerto Montt, how to cover the final stretch into town, and what it takes to make the whole journey overland.

Balmaceda Airport's new terminal

One point worth clearing up: Coyhaique did not build an airport somewhere else. The project expanded and upgraded Balmaceda Airport, which sits in the small town of Balmaceda, about 50 minutes by car from the city. Chilean media called it the "new Coyhaique airport", but it is the same airport; what opened is a far larger passenger terminal.

The work is part of the Ministry of Public Works' "Red Aeroportuaria Austral" concession, the first concession-based project in the region, awarded to the Consorcio Austral de Aeropuertos for 25 years. Construction began in May 2023. In April 2026 the ministry reported 85% progress and was still targeting the first half of 2027, but things moved faster: the terminal was handed over for provisional operation in August 2026 and, after a trial period, opened to passengers on 5 October 2026.

The terminal also has a VIP lounge and shops and food outlets featuring local cuisine and crafts. Press reports put capacity at more than 1 million passengers a year, while the ministry projects demand of 1.2 million. Investment exceeds US$50 million, according to 2026 press coverage.

A less visible change may matter more to travellers. Chile's Airports Directorate is upgrading the movement area and installing a low-visibility approach and landing aid. The goal is fewer weather-related flight suspensions, which are common in Patagonia.

International flights? The terminal is rated for international travel, but there are no confirmed international routes yet. Work is under way with Brazil and Argentina to bring international flights in during the 2027 summer, although these routes are not confirmed. For now, visitors from abroad connect in Santiago or Puerto Montt.

Flights to Coyhaique from Santiago and Puerto Montt

The airport code is BBA (Balmaceda). These are the routes available today:

  • From Santiago: LATAM and Sky fly nonstop in about 2 hours 20 minutes.
  • From Puerto Montt: Sky, JetSMART and LATAM fly the route in about 1 hour 22 minutes. According to Skyscanner, there were around 38 weekly frequencies in February 2026.
  • From Temuco: Sky has announced a seasonal nonstop route for the 2027 summer. Check the details with the airline.

If you plan to fly JetSMART from Santiago, check on its website that the nonstop Balmaceda route is running: sources disagree.

For planning: it is just over 2 hours by air from Santiago to Balmaceda and, from there, about 50 minutes by car to Coyhaique.

From the airport to Coyhaique: the final stretch

Balmaceda is about 50 minutes by car from Coyhaique. It is a short drive, but in Patagonia it pays to have your transfer sorted before you land, especially if you arrive on the last flight of the day or in winter, when snow and frost are frequent.

You may spot Teniente Vidal Airfield on the map, right next to the city, some 4–5 km away. It is a small airfield with a runway of about 1,548 m, and it is not where commercial flights from Santiago and Puerto Montt land. There is no official plan to move or replace it.

Getting to Coyhaique overland

Coyhaique is almost 1,700 km from Santiago, more than 30 hours by car, according to Emol. There are two ways to drive it:

  1. Along the Carretera Austral (Route 7): south from Puerto Montt, with ferries along the way, such as the short Caleta La Arena–Caleta Puelche crossing (about 30 minutes) and Hornopirén–Caleta Gonzalo (about 5 hours, in season). It is the most scenic option and best done without hurrying. Our Carretera Austral road trip guide has the details.
  2. Through Argentina: the alternative for those who would rather skip the ferries.

A combination that works well: fly into Balmaceda, rent a vehicle and explore the region by road. That puts the Marble Caves, about 220 km south, within reach, or in winter the El Fraile ski area, 30 minutes from the city.

Frequently asked questions

Which airport serves Coyhaique?

Balmaceda Airport (BBA), about 50 minutes by car from the city. Since 5 October 2026 it has operated with a new 12,391 m² terminal.

How long is the flight from Santiago to Coyhaique?

The nonstop LATAM or Sky flight to Balmaceda takes about 2 hours 20 minutes. Add about 50 minutes by car into the city.

Are there international flights to Balmaceda?

Not at the moment. The terminal is rated for international flights, and work is under way with Brazil and Argentina to bring in flights during the 2027 summer, but these routes are not confirmed.

Can you drive to Coyhaique from Santiago?

Yes. It is almost 1,700 km and more than 30 hours behind the wheel, via the Carretera Austral with its ferries or through Argentina.
