In Aysén, clear water runs down from the Andes through open valleys of forest and pasture. Fly fishing in Patagonia, Chile here means wadeable rivers, wide lakes and trout taken on dry flies, nymphs and hopper patterns. This guide covers what you need for the 2026–2027 season: official dates, the fishing licence, the rivers and the rules.

The main species are rainbow trout, brown trout and brook trout (fontinalis). Some rivers also hold Chinook salmon, which has its own season. If you are still planning the trip, start with our guide on how to get to Coyhaique.

Aysén fishing season 2026–2027: key dates

The recreational fishing season in the Aysén Region opened on Monday 12 October 2026 for all rivers, lakes and freshwater bodies. Sernapesca, Chile's fisheries service, marked the opening with an event in the main square of Coyhaique. Dates depend on the type of water:

WaterMandatory catch and releaseHarvest allowedMandatory catch and release
Rivers12 Oct to 30 Nov 20261 Dec 2026 to 28 Feb 2027: up to 3 fish or 15 kg per angler per day1 Mar to 6 May 2027
Lakes12 Oct to 9 Nov 202610 Nov 2026 to 1 Apr 2027: same limit2 Apr to 6 May 2027
Chinook salmon12 Oct 2026 to 15 Jan 2027, with a maximum of 2 fish per angler per day

The season closes on 6 May 2027, and the closed season runs from 7 May to 11 October. According to Patagonia Fishing Report, these dates match Exempt Resolution No. 893 (8 April 2025), which amends Resolution 3004/2024 and applies to 2025–2027.

Some spawning areas are closed all year, even to catch and release (Diario Oficial, 21 April 2025). Before fishing a new stretch, check that it is not one of them.

Fishing licence: how to get it and what it costs

You need a valid Sernapesca licence to fish. You can get it online at pescarecreativa.sernapesca.cl and show it printed or on your phone. According to ChileAtiende, it can also be obtained in person at Sernapesca offices. Non-resident foreigners apply with their passport or national ID card.

Fees are set in UF (Chile's inflation-indexed unit) by Law 20.256, article 6. The amount in pesos is calculated with the UF value on the last day of the previous year.

Foreign tourists (all waters)

  • One week: 0.5 UF.
  • One month: 1.0 UF.
  • One year: 1.5 UF.

Chileans and resident foreigners

  • Inland waters: 0.1 UF for one week, 0.2 UF for one month and 0.3 UF for one year.
  • Sea waters: 0.2 UF for one year.
  • Sea and inland waters: 0.4 UF for one year.

Chileans and resident foreigners under 12, over 65 or listed in the National Disability Registry are exempt.

Rivers for fly fishing in Chilean Patagonia

Simpson River

The Simpson flows past Coyhaique and through the Río Simpson National Reserve, at km 37 of the Coyhaique–Puerto Aysén road. Where it meets the Mañihuales it becomes the Aysén River. CONAF, the national parks agency, describes it as the fourth best in the world for fly fishing. It is a promotional claim, but it shows the river's reputation among anglers.

Mañihuales River

The Mañihuales runs through a wide pastoral valley with clear green water. Outfitters fish it with grasshopper and beetle patterns and with nymphs. It is a tributary of the Simpson.

Emperador Guillermo River

A small river of about 18 miles (around 29 km), it flows down from the Villa Ortega area into the Mañihuales, right by the Carretera Austral. It is fished on foot, wading, with dry flies and nymphs. It holds brown and rainbow trout, with plenty of grasshoppers, stoneflies and caddis. Almost the whole river has road access, except for a canyon of about 2 miles in its lower section (Deneki Outdoors). The turn-off into its valley leaves the Carretera Austral about 13 km south of Villa Mañihuales, on route X-50 towards Villa Ortega. Near the Durán Bridge the river cuts a canyon with vertical walls.

Other rivers in the area

Outfitters also mention the La Paloma, Aysén, Ñirehuao and Baker rivers. To combine fishing with a wider trip, our Carretera Austral road trip guide will help you plan the route.

Rules on the water and Didymo prevention

  • Only artificial lures are allowed.
  • One rod per person.
  • You must carry a valid licence.
  • Setting nets in fresh water is a crime.

To prevent the spread of Didymo, Sernapesca asks anglers to clean, wash and dry their gear after every fishing day. It is a simple habit that protects the rivers you came to fish. For questions about the season, call 800 320 032.

The harvest limit is a maximum, not a target: during harvest periods you can still release everything you catch.

Frequently asked questions

When does the 2026 fishing season start in Aysén?

It opened on Monday 12 October 2026 and closes on 6 May 2027. On rivers, harvest is only allowed between 1 December 2026 and 28 February 2027.

Can foreign tourists fish in Chile?

Yes. You need a recreational fishing licence, which you can get with your passport or ID card. It costs 0.5 UF for a week, 1.0 UF for a month or 1.5 UF for a year, and is valid for all waters.

Can I fish for Chinook salmon?

Yes, from 12 October 2026 to 15 January 2027, with a maximum of 2 fish per angler per day.

Where can I fly fish near Coyhaique?

The Simpson flows past the city itself and through the Río Simpson National Reserve, at km 37 of the road to Puerto Aysén. North along the Carretera Austral you will find the Mañihuales and the Emperador Guillermo.
