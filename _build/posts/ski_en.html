In winter, Coyhaique slows down. The hills turn white, chimney smoke drifts upward and, 30 minutes from town, a road through snow-laden lenga forest climbs to the slopes. The El Fraile ski area is a small mountain centre close to the city, with Patagonia as its backdrop.

This guide covers where it is, how to get there, its runs and lifts, when it opens, what it offers in summer and which details you should check before heading up, because several of them change from one season to the next.

Where El Fraile is and how to get there

El Fraile is 30 minutes from Coyhaique, up Cerro El Fraile on the road to Lake Pollux. The ski area sits at around 1,599 m above sea level. In winter it is used for snow activities, and in summer for hiking and downhill mountain biking.

In midwinter the gravel section can be snow-covered. Go up in a suitable vehicle, drive carefully and check road conditions before you set off.

If you are coming from outside the region, the usual route is to fly into Balmaceda Airport and continue by road to Coyhaique. Our guide on how to get to Coyhaique explains the options.

Runs and lifts

El Fraile runs 2 surface lifts: a button lift and a T-bar. Do not expect chairlifts or large facilities; this is a ski area on a local scale.

  • Gradients: from 8° to 35°, so there is terrain for beginners as well as for those looking for something steeper.
  • Run length: from 600 to 3,200 m.
  • Number of runs: this varies by source. Chileanski mentions "5 runs" and Skiresort.info lists "5 km of slopes". Treat these as a guide.

Skiresort.info shows the area as "temporarily closed", without saying since when. Before planning your trip, confirm that El Fraile is operating that season.

Season and opening hours

The season generally runs from June to September, snow permitting. At a season launch, reported by Radio Revelación in an undated article, there was talk of extending it into early October. One package seller listed 5 July as the start of the 2026 season.

According to the same launch, the area opens Tuesday to Sunday: you drive up between 8:00 and 13:00 and come down between 14:00 and 20:00. Other sources give hours of 8:30 to 17:30. Since timings can change with snow and weather, check before you go.

More snow: snowshoeing and winter in Coyhaique

Skiing is not the only way to enjoy the snow in Aysén. One operator offers snowshoe walks in 2026, with a minimum of 2 people. It is a good alternative if you do not ski or would like to walk through snowy forest at your own pace.

Winter in Coyhaique is cold, with frequent frost and snow. In August 2026 a cold snap and heavy snowfall led the municipality to set up an emergency shelter. If you travel in those months, pack thermal layers, check the forecast and bear in mind that rural roads can be difficult. Outside the snow season, El Fraile is used for hiking and downhill mountain biking, and the region opens up to other plans, such as a Carretera Austral road trip or a visit to the Marble Caves.

Frequently asked questions

How far is El Fraile from Coyhaique?

30 minutes from Coyhaique, on the road to Lake Pollux.

When does El Fraile open?

Generally from June to September, depending on snow, Tuesday to Sunday. Confirm each season's opening date before you travel.

Can you visit El Fraile in summer?

Yes. In winter the area is used for snow activities, and in summer for hiking and downhill mountain biking.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Gradients range from 8° to 35°, so there are gentle sections as well as steeper ones. The lifts are surface tows, which is worth bearing in mind if it is your first time.
